# User guide

What each part of Anza does, written for support, product and anyone demoing the app. The in-app guide (`/dashboard/guide`) covers the same ground for founders.

## 1. Getting in

1. **Sign up** with email and password, or with Google.
2. **Confirm your email** from the link Anza sends. Didn't get it? Check spam, then use "Resend confirmation email" on the sign-in page.
3. **Onboarding** asks about you and your first business: name, country, industry, stage, budget.
4. You land on the **Overview** of your business.

Forgot your password? "Forgot?" on the sign-in page sends a reset link. Open it in the same browser you requested it from; it expires after about an hour.

## 2. The workspace

A **business** is a workspace. Everything inside it (plans, tasks, documents, chat) is shared with its team. You can own several businesses and switch between them from the top bar.

The sidebar groups the tools by stage. Anza opens the group that suits your business's stage first.

### Always visible

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Business health, next actions, risks, weekly focus plan and recent activity. A score appears only when there is enough data to compute it |
| **Copilot** | Chat with six AI specialists who read your workspace. They remember facts, goals and decisions. When they suggest a change (a task, a milestone), nothing happens until you click to confirm it |

### Discover

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Idea Lab** | Generate ideas from your skills, studies or problems around you. Each is scored on six criteria you can see and adjust. Turn the best one into a business. Ideas are personal: they are not shared with a team |
| **Market Research** | Ten research topics. With web research (Pro), findings come with sources and dates. Without it, the report says plainly that nothing was checked against outside sources |
| **Validation** | List what must be true for the business to work (assumptions), then log real evidence: interviews, surveys, experiments. Twelve analyses help you decide what to test |

### Plan

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Business Model** | Business Model Canvas and Lean Canvas. Autosaves, keeps versions, AI can suggest or review |
| **Business Plan** | 17 sections, written and improved one at a time. Versions, export to PDF or Word, and a read-only share link (Pro) |
| **Finance** | Start-up costs, monthly projections, break-even, unit economics, runway, seven calculators and live currency conversion |

### Run

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Projects** | Tasks on a board, list or calendar; milestones; comments. Describe an objective and Anza proposes a plan you can accept |
| **Marketing** | Eleven strategy analyses, a content writer for six formats, and a content calendar |
| **Documents** | Upload PDFs, Word, Excel, CSV and text files (up to 25 MB). The AI uses them as context. Organise into folders and summarise |

### Grow

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Opportunities** | Grants, accelerators and competitions curated by the Anza team, each with its source and the date it was checked. Bookmark and track applications |
| **Funding** | Funding readiness and a pitch deck |
| **Analytics** | Enter your real numbers (revenue, customers, burn, visitors). These are marked "Tracked". AI projections are marked "AI projection" and start from your tracked numbers |
| **Legal** | Orientation on company forms, registration and compliance for your country. General guidance only: always confirm with a local professional |

### Workspace

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Businesses** | All the businesses you own or belong to |
| **Team** | Invite people, manage roles, team chat |
| **Settings** | Your profile, the business details, brand colour, language, plan usage |

## 3. Teams

**Inviting.** Team → Invite, enter an email and a role. Anza creates a private link you can copy or share by WhatsApp or email. Only someone signed in with that exact, confirmed email can accept it.

**Roles.**

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including deleting the business. There is one owner, the person who created it |
| Administrator | Change settings and manage the team (except owners and other administrators), and edit everything |
| Editor | Create and edit plans, tasks, documents and so on |
| Advisor | Read everything, comment, and chat |
| Viewer | Read only. Can read the chat but not post |

**Team chat.** A General channel and one per project. Replies, emoji reactions, @mentions, unread badges, who is online and typing, search, and "turn this message into a task". Start a message with `@copilot` to ask the AI in front of the team.

- Inside a business, the **owner's plan** decides which tools the team can use and how many people can join. Each person's **own plan** decides their monthly AI allowance.
- The allowance resets on the 1st of each month. Settings shows how much is left.
- Payments are not connected yet. Plans are assigned by the Anza team; the list prices in the app are indicative.

## 5. Languages

English and French. Change it in Settings or from the language switch; AI answers follow the chosen language. Some older pages (legal, funding, analytics, settings, team, guide, admin and the sign-in pages) are still English only.

## 6. What Anza will not do

- It will not invent statistics, laws, fees or funding programmes. When it does not know, it says so.
- It will not change your data without a click from you.
- It does not give legal, tax or financial advice. It gives orientation and tells you who to ask.
- It does not collect payments or move money.

## 7. Frequently asked by users

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| "I didn't get the confirmation or reset email" | Check spam; wait a minute; resend. If it still doesn't come, contact support: the email service may be limited |
| "The reset link says it expired" | Request a new one and open it in the same browser |
| "I can't edit anything" | Your role in this business is Advisor or Viewer. Ask the owner |
| "The AI says I've used my allowance" | It resets on the 1st. Ask the Anza team about a higher plan |
| "Can someone outside my team see my plan?" | No, unless you create a share link. You can revoke it at any time |
| "How do I delete my account?" | Contact support (see the privacy policy) |
